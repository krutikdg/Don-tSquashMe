# DontSquashMe
A chaotic little survival game where your only goal is to stay alive for as long as possible.  Dodge flying hazards, react to attacks coming from different directions, and use your ultimate survival technique, becoming a crumb.

## How to play?

- WASD/ Arrow Keys to move around the desk.
- SPACE to activate crumb mode to temporarily avoid danger.
- Survive to increase your score.
- Beat your best score 

Made with Godot 4 for the Jam Gam Game Jam =]

Theme: I'm just a little guy.
